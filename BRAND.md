# Lions Club Pulheim – Brand Sheet (Entwurf)

Direction A: "Abtei, Navy & Gold". Privilege through tradition, ritual and restraint.

## Colours
| Name | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Nachtblau | `#0E2240` | Hero, footer, dark sections |
| Abteiblau | `#16315A` | Raised surfaces on dark |
| Elfenbein | `#F6F1E7` | Main light background |
| Papier | `#FBF8F2` | Alternate light background |
| Gold | `#C29A4E` | Lines, ornament, accents on dark only |
| Altgold | `#7A5C1E` | Accent text on light (AA contrast) |
| Tinte | `#1B2333` | Body text on light |

## Typefaces (self-hosted, OFL, no Google request – DSGVO)
- **Cormorant Garamond** – headlines, numerals, quotes. Engraved, concert-programme feel.
- **Jost** – body, navigation, labels. Futura lineage, quiet and precise.

## Voice
Ruhig, selbstbewusst, einladend. Wir laden ein, wir werben nicht. "Sie", nie "du".

## Signature element
The Romanesque portal: nested round arches after Abtei Brauweiler. Used once large
(hero), echoed as arch-shaped image frames. Nowhere else.

## Rules
- Gold is a line colour, never a fill for large areas.
- No Lions emblem until the club confirms usage under the Lions International brand rules.
- No member names or portraits without written consent.
- One motion moment: the portal draws itself on load.
